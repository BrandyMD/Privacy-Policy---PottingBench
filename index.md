  <h1>Privacy Policy for PottingBench</h1>
  <p class="date"><em>Last updated: November 1, 2026</em></p>

  <p>This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>PottingBench</strong> ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We prioritize transparent, offline-first design and do not require account creation or personal profile registration to access our workbench utilities.</p>

  <h2>1. Local Processing & Device Hardware Access</h2>
  <p>PottingBench functions locally on your device. The calculations, custom recipe batches, and image-alignment tools execute on-device without transmitting your data to remote cloud servers:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Saved Configurations & Unit Preferences:</strong> Your active preferences (such as Metric versus US Standard units, selected substrate mixes, active cutting habits, and heft benchmarks) are stored exclusively in your device's local storage (e.g., <code>localStorage</code>). We do not collect, transmit, or synchronize your bench configurations to external servers.</li>
    <li><strong>Camera & Photo Uploads (Stem Doctor):</strong> The app provides an alignment canvas reticle designed to help you align node cuts. When you capture a photograph using your device camera or upload an image from your photo gallery, that image is processed purely inside your device's local memory. Photos are never uploaded, stored on remote databases, or shared with third parties.</li>
    <li><strong>Screen Wake Lock API:</strong> To keep your screen visible while your hands are active at the workbench, the app may request permission to keep your screen illuminated (Wake Lock). This feature does not monitor, collect, or store any behavioral, visual, or biometric data.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising</h2>
  <p>To support continuous technical maintenance and understand general application performance, the app may integrate third-party services provided by Google LLC. These services collect certain operational data automatically:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Google AdMob:</strong> We may use Google AdMob to display banner or interstitial advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), coarse approximate location inferred from IP addresses, and anonymized ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant, contextual advertisements and prevent fraudulent traffic.</li>
    <li><strong>Google Analytics for Firebase:</strong> We utilize Firebase Analytics to evaluate diagnostic stability, app retention, and user navigation trends across tabs. Firebase automatically collects pseudonymous analytics identifiers, device hardware details, operating system versions, and standard in-app session metrics.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>For detailed information on how Google manages and secures data gathered through SDK integrations, please review <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites" target="_blank" rel="noopener">How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services</a> and the official <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>3. Data Retention and User Control</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Advertising ID Management:</strong> You can reset or restrict your device's advertising identifier at any time through your Android system settings (under <em>Settings &gt; Google &gt; Ads</em> or <em>Settings &gt; Privacy &gt; Ads</em>).</li>
    <li><strong>Clearing Cached App Data:</strong> You have absolute control over your local data. You can delete all locally cached substrate preferences, saved measurements, and interface settings at any time by clearing the application storage via <em>Settings &gt; Apps &gt; PottingBench &gt; Storage &gt; Clear Storage/Cache</em>, or by uninstalling the application from your device.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Children’s Privacy</h2>
  <p>PottingBench is intended for general audiences seeking practical horticultural and houseplant care guidance. The app does not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.</p>

  <h2>5. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>
  <p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to accommodate evolving privacy regulations, operational requirements, or platform updates. Any modifications will be posted to this page alongside an updated effective revision date.</p>
